# Trove workflow audit — 5 November 2026

This is a refinement of the established white/charcoal interface. The audit used fresh Chrome/Playwright captures of the actual local application, with desktop (1440px) and phone (390px) views, plus 1366×768 purchase/consent states. The full local screenshot walkthrough is `.local/whole-workflow/report.html`; original files and state/ARIA manifests are beside it. Failed asynchronous captures were rejected; they are not evidence of completed states.

The key findings were broken phone action layout, URL-first sample tables, no sample inspection from research, redundant headings/labels and empty seller metrics. The implementation corrects those while retaining exact price, scope, freshness, attribution, licence, payment/delivery status and explicit approval. A closed purchase now offers a useful recovery action instead of implying data is still on its way.

Research method: Impeccable distillation/craft, Product Design screenshot-first audit and research, plus Layers orientation, conceptual-model, interaction-flow and surface guidance. [Layers](https://github.com/jamiemill/layers-skills) informed task analysis and terminology, not a new checklist or a replacement design system. A public scan found no independent Trove customer feedback; marketplace discussions and SDK issues were weak contextual evidence, not user validation. No customer interview or usability study is claimed.

## Validation and limits

Strict types and production build pass. All 82 backend tests passed for scope, ownership, dispute review, refund-worker gates, accounting and external refund reconciliation. Clean-database CI passed all **43 browser tests**, strict types, production build and dependency audit (zero vulnerabilities), including the corrected hidden-option assertion and closed-purchase recovery. All **27 hosted read-only checks** also passed; exact release evidence is in AUDIT.md.

The independent finish reviewer returned **disposition: ship** for the supplied interface states, with no material fixes. Its bounded closed-purchase delta check also returned **ship**, with recovery confirmed at desktop, phone and short-laptop sizes. Documentation handoff preserves existing tokens and updates the sample/disclosure, inline-host and support patterns. No new detector exception was added. The existing narrow Inter exception remains intentional for the pinned reference; inherited miniature utility captions were not promoted to body-text patterns.

Screenshots supplement keyboard, focus, responsive, reduced-motion and Axe checks; they are not an accessibility certification. Local TEST_ONLY purchased/cancelled states use actual stored public FDA rows and never fabricate a production payment. Filing a support case leaves capture and access intact; only trusted review followed by authoritative processor completion refunds. No new external refund, seller payout or signed webhook delivery is established here. Google sign-in, email verification and password recovery were owner-confirmed previously, not re-proven by these captures.

Actual ChatGPT host rendering requires refreshing the Trove app connection and a real client session. The existing connection returned a missing refresh-token error during the post-deploy profile check; owner reconnection remains necessary. A custom MCP alone does not enable native in-chat checkout. The built view says PayPal approval opens on Trove. No new service account, paid resource or key was introduced. Optional AI generation and full external payment/support acceptance remain separate from deterministic search and local host tests.


## Research brief and source map

The product is Trove; the audience is research buyers and people using their own AI agent. This scan focuses on the current hackathon build and immediate purchase, setup and support friction. The strongest evidence is the owner's reported blocked preview/purchase flow and the current captured application, not a large body of external customer reviews. Buyers need to assess usable evidence before deciding to pay; this is a design inference supported by the observed preview gap. They also need permission failures to lead to consent rather than token/config editing. Empty statistics and repeated labels compete with those decisions without helping them. The immediate work therefore improves evidence, recovery and action hierarchy; future product direction still needs observed customer use.

| Priority | Problem and user goal | Evidence / frequency signal | Confidence | Product move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Inspect useful data and an exact quote before granting purchase access | Owner's ChatGPT error plus local read-token acceptance; one reported workflow, not a measured user rate | High for reproduced scope behavior | Published fixed samples and read-scoped quotes; purchase/support actions return explicit OAuth challenges |
| P1 | Report an actual delivery issue without authorizing an immediate refund | Explicit owner requirement; local case/worker lifecycle tests | High for implemented behavior; real refund operation still separate | Human evidence review, immutable history, separate approval and confirmed processor completion |
| P2 | Use the dashboard's agent action on a phone | Steps 5/29, reproduced at 390px | High for this viewport | Put the link in the proper grid column with normal width |
| P2 | Assess samples before approving the research plan | Steps 7/10/33; current UI observation | High for missing action; value of samples is an inference | Reuse the sample view in research and show useful fields before raw references |
| P2 | Recover from cancelled payment without waiting for impossible delivery | Step 37; local controlled voided state | High for copy/state mismatch | Closed-purchase state and explicit source-review recovery |

[Layers](https://github.com/jamiemill/layers-skills) supplied conceptual-model and interaction-flow methods, not validation of Trove demand. [MCP SDK issue 1582](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/typescript-sdk/issues/1582) supplies a concrete report of progressive-authorization friction elsewhere; it is not proof of Trove's exact bug or its prevalence. [Try Before You Buy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08874) studies evaluating data before purchase, including algorithm-performance evidence; this supports investigating evidence before purchase but does not establish that Trove's two-row previews predict downstream research quality. Public searches covered Reddit, Hacker News, Stack Overflow/GitHub and exact Trove name/URL; no independent Trove feedback was found. No internal customer-support corpus was accessed.

**Now:** the reproduced layout, sample-access, permission, closed-state and refund-policy problems are implemented and tested. **Next quarter:** observe where real buyers abandon preview → quote → approval before adding more domains or dashboard metrics. **Needs deeper research:** willingness to pay, whether a fixed sample predicts value, recurring professional workflows and real provider economics. Those questions cannot be answered from this UI audit.
