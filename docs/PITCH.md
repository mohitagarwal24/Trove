# Hackathon pitch and supply disclosure

Trove lets research agents discover and buy scoped information with explicit human approval. The demonstration should show an actual Gemini plan, genuine PayPal Sandbox approval, verified delivery, a source-linked receipt and independently tracked provider earnings.

**Supply disclosure:** Northstar Regulatory, Atlas Safety and Meridian Devices are illustrative provider identities created for the hackathon; no real provider partnership or onboarding agreement exists. Their catalogue products are prepared from publicly available openFDA records. The records themselves are real, and upstream provenance and reuse rights are preserved. Prices illustrate payment for preparation and delivery, not exclusive access to FDA information.

The application uses ordinary paid-product contracts and storage for these offers. It has no simulated payment, shared demo login, fake model response or fabricated data generator. Sandbox identifies the payment environment accurately. Future onboarded providers use the same account, publication, review, quote and delivery path.

## Three-minute walkthrough

- Show a regulatory research question and the source catalogue. Explain the supply disclosure above before presenting payment.
- Run real AI research with a bounded task allowance. Inspect selected fields, filters, records, source version and exact price. A task allowance is policy, not a PayPal wallet or mandate.
- Approve the exact amount using a PayPal Sandbox personal buyer. Show delivery only after confirmed capture and inspect actual payment references.
- Retrieve the same result again without another purchase. Inspect source attribution, receipt and usage terms.
- Show a real provider profile, pending review/upload flow and immutable offer edit. Earnings remain owed unless an actual payout item succeeds.
- Show an agent token and eight bounded MCP tools. Explain that agents can prepare purchases but humans approve them.

## Tracks and evidence

The strongest fit is the main PayPal challenge through AI/automation, business solutions and an agent-oriented consumer purchase experience. These are product directions, not a claim that each inspiration heading is a separate award track. Render hosting and optional Elastic metadata search are implemented; sponsor award eligibility must be checked against the current event rules and demonstrated integration requirements. Merely reading resources, adding a Postman collection or importing an SDK does not qualify for a prize.

Do not claim validated demand, exclusive data, medical advice, production marketplace approval, successful external payout, or successful provider calls without recorded evidence. A good follow-up business direction is paid regulatory-data preparation for analyst teams, measured by time saved and repeat usage.
