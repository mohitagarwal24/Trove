# Trove

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers and developers discover and compare scoped data offers, then approve an exact-price purchase. Sellers manage offer versions and review earnings. External agents use scoped, revocable access.

## Product Purpose

Make research data discoverable, purchasable with explicit human approval, and traceable to source-linked receipts. This implementation demonstrates the mechanism using synthetic fixtures for the PayPal AI Hackathon.

## Operating Context

Discover → compare or research brief → immutable quote → human approval → delivery checks → payment capture → purchased results and receipt. Payment, delivery, and settlement have independent states. Guided demonstration, live AI, PayPal Sandbox, and simulation are explicitly distinguished.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the implemented workflows, prices, permissions, provenance, and synthetic-data disclosures. Keep hosting and integrations within free options. Never fabricate providers, verification, payments, or AI calls. Existing Next.js application and Render deployment are the implementation targets. Architecture and routine implementation decisions are delegated by the user.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Trove. The user requests a professional, distinctive identity replacing the generic green theme, usable at normal browser zoom. The sidebar's “Your agent. Better informed.” content must remain reachable on shorter laptop screens.

## Evidence on Hand

Eight synthetic offers, three fictional providers, real application flows, local fonts, and existing product documentation under docs/. No customer testimonials or real data-provider relationships are established.

## Product Principles

- The user approves each exact purchase.
- Source scope and licence precede payment.
- Demonstrations identify synthetic data and simulated money movement.
- Layout and interaction serve a working research tool.
